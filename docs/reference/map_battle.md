# The map battle

When battle animations are off, a fight is played on the map itself: the two units' small battle sprites face each other, swing, and the HP windows count down. The same sprites and helpers also show a few map actions and scripted scenes. All of it is bank `$9E`. Per-routine details: [routines/battle.md](routines/battle.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## Files

| File | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| [map_battle.asm](../../engine/battle/map_battle.asm) | `procMapBattle` and everything it uses: the combatants' sprites (`aBattleMapSpriteData`), the rounds, ballista bolts, quotes, the level up banner and stat gains, the gold and item popups, the clean-up; the scripted critical's effect |
| [map_action_animations.asm](../../engine/battle/map_action_animations.asm) | village raid (`procVillageRaid`), return home (`procReturnHome`), holy weapon shine (`procHolyWeaponShine`), give gold (`procGiveGold`), and rewards of an action without a battle (`procMapActionRewards`) |
| [map_battle_hud.asm](../../engine/battle/map_battle_hud.asm) | the HP windows (magic placement, idle/busy checks, palettes `aMapBattleHPWindowPalettePointers` and accents), the five-step gauge helpers (`rlDrawMapMenuTwoRowGauge`, `rsDrawMapMenuGaugeRow`, `aMapMenuGaugeTiles`), the experience window and text box (bank `$85`), and `procMapExperienceWindowEffect` |
| [cutscenes.asm](../../engine/events/cutscenes.asm) | the warp away/in animations, the Belhalla mages, and the starters for scripted map battles that events run |

The magic effects (bank `$9F`) are in [map_battle_magic.asm](../../engine/battle/map_battle_magic.asm) (the shared machinery and the data each effect loads) and [map_magic_effects.asm](../../engine/battle/map_magic_effects.asm) (one starter per spell and its steps); see "Magic effects" below.

Data: `aMapAction` (`$7F454A`) and `wMapBattleMode` (`$7F4C87`) in [include/WRAM.h](../../include/WRAM.h); the rounds come from `aBattleRoundsData`, written by the battle calculation.

## Starting it

`rlStartMapBattleAnimation` creates `procMapBattle` in the first free proc slot. `wMapBattleMode` says why:

- **0, a battle**: `rlStartMapBattle` (bank `$81`) when the animations are off.
- **1, the opening's demo battles**: `rlASMCStartDemoMapBattle`, run by the opening's event script.
- **2, a scripted battle from an event**: `rlStartScriptedMapBattleAnimation` (two units, e.g. Arvis attacking Sigurd) and `rlStartSoloMapBattleAnimation` (one unit, Silvia's dance in chapter 4). These fill the battle action structs themselves.

Only mode 0 changes HP (`rlApplyMapBattleRoundHP`).

## How a battle runs

The proc's script, `aProcMapBattleCode`, is the battle's outline. Most steps are a routine that sets proc header bit `$0002` when it has something to do and bit `$0004` when it is done, and the script waits on those bits.

1. **Set up** (`rlProcMapBattleInit`, `rlInitMapBattleCombatants`): pauses the map's sprite and tile animations, and fills `aMapAction` from the two action structs: each side's battle sprite class (`rlGetMapBattleClassSprite`: some classes have a female sprite, and Seliph as a Knight Lord has his own), weapon type, tile position and facing (`rlGetMapBattleFacing`).
2. **Camera** (`rlMapBattleSetCameraTarget`, `rlMapBattleMoveCamera`): scrolls the map until both units are on screen.
3. **Sprites**: the attacker's then the defender's battle sprites appear (`rlShowMapBattleAttacker`, `rlShowMapBattleDefender`) over their hidden map sprites.
4. **Battle quote** (`rlMapBattleStartBattleQuote`): when `wBattleQuotePointer` is set, a dialogue box; afterwards the sprite tiles it covered are decompressed and uploaded again.
5. **HP windows** (`rlProcMapBattleOpenHPWindows`).
6. **Rounds** (`rlMapBattleNextRound` and the steps after it), until the entry `$FFFF` in `aBattleRoundsData`: each round picks its sprite scripts by side and by its hit, miss and critical bits (`rsGetMapBattleRoundScripts`), may fire a ballista bolt (`procMapBattleBallistaBolt`, when a side is one of the four ballistician classes) or a map magic effect (bank `$9F`), and applies the HP change. A Pavise activation (entry `$02FE`) first shows the shield (`procMapBattlePavise`). When a strike lands (`rlMapBattleRoundStep`), the struck side recoils (`rlMapBattleRunRecoil`) and flashes white (`rlMapBattleRunHitFlash`), with the hit sound (`rlMapBattlePlayHitSound`: `$94` for a fatal strike, `$93` ranged, `$91`/`$93` otherwise, `$4E` more for a critical) or the no-damage sound `$95`; the HP changes at the same time (`rlApplyMapBattleRoundHP`). A defeated unit cries out (`rlMapBattlePlayDefeatCry`: sound `$21`-`$23` by voice, `$24`/`$25` for a Child).
   A combatant moved a tile off a shared tile for the battle (`wBattleStatus` bit 2) slides out 16 pixels before the rounds and back after (`rlMapBattleSlideShiftedUnitOut`/`Back`). After a Warp, its arrival plays at the destination when that is on screen (`procMapBattleWarpArrival`).
7. **Death quote** (`rlMapBattleStartDeathQuote`).
8. **Experience** (`rlMapBattleOpenExperienceWindow`), then **level up** (`rlMapBattleStartLevelUpBanner`, `rlMapBattleStatGains`, with `rlPlayLevelUpJingle` and `rlPlayStatGainSound`).
9. **Popups**, each with the music lowered and a jingle: gold obtained (ゴールド　てにいれた！), gold stolen (ゴールド　ぬすまれた), an item obtained (を　てにいれた！), a weapon broken (は　こわれてしまった).
10. **End** (`rlProcMapBattleEnd`, `rlEndMapBattleRestoreMap`): the camera returns to the cursor, the windows close, the palettes and the units' map sprites come back, and canto is checked.

## Magic effects

A tome or staff plays an effect over a background of its own. The effect number is the item ID minus Fire (`$3E`), so 0 is Fire and 41 BrokenStaffA. `rlInitMapBattleCombatants` stores each side's number (`wMapMagicAttackerEffect`, `wMapMagicDefenderEffect`; magic swords get one through `rlGetMagicSwordMapEffect`: at range the Fire, Earth, Thunder and Wind Swords and the Light Brand play Elfire, Nosferatu, Elthunder, Elwind and Light, and the Berserk, Silence and Sleep Swords their staff's), and `rlSetMapMagicFlags` sets `wMapMagicFlags`: 0 when nothing plays, `$8000` in demo and scripted battles, 1 on the castle screen, else 1 plus `$0100`, `$0200` or `$0400` when the units are within `aMapMagicEdgeMargins` tiles of the left, right or top edge. All of it is in `include/WRAM.h` from `$7F5089`.

For each round, the battle's script:

1. `rlSelectMapMagicEffectForRound`: `wMapMagicEffect` = the acting side's number.
2. `rlQueueMapMagicBackgroundTiles`, `...Tilemap`, `...Palette` (queued before the rounds) decompress the background from `aMapBattleMagicBackgrounds`. Meteor and Valflame have a second tilemap for a target on the right of the screen.
3. `rlUploadMapMagicBackgroundTiles`, `...TilesRest`, `...Tilemap`, `rlCopyMapMagicBackgroundColors`, then `rlLoadMapMagicSprites` and `rlUploadMapMagicSpriteTiles` (`aMapBattleMagicSprites`).
4. `rlStartMapMagicEffect` saves the screen registers and creates `procMapMagicEffect`.
5. The battler's sprite script reaches `ACTIVE_SPRITE_START_MAP_MAGIC` (command `$10`), which sets `wMapMagicStart`. The proc then runs the spell's starter from `aMapMagicEffectStarters` (`rlStartMapMagicFire` ... `rlStartMapMagicBrokenStaff`) and ends. The script waits at `ACTIVE_SPRITE_WAIT_MAP_MAGIC` (command `$11`) until the effect sets `wMapMagicDone`, usually after fading the colours back (`rsFadeBackMapMagicColors`).
6. `rlEndMapMagicEffect` frees the procs and HDMA entries the effect created (`aMapMagicProcList`, `aMapMagicHDMAList`, filled by `rsCreateMapMagicProc` and `rsCreateMapMagicHDMA`), and `rlRestoreMapMagicScreenRegisters` puts the screen back.

The starters share effects: Heal's (sound `$71`, `procMapMagicHealingStaff`) also plays for Naga, Loptous, the broken fire tomes, Charm and Valkyrie; Mend, Recover and Physic use the same proc with their own sound; Elthunder serves Bolting, Wind Elwind, Warp Rescue. The Belhalla mages' meteors use Meteor's (effect 4). Shared bank `$9F` machinery in [map_battle_magic.asm](../../engine/battle/map_battle_magic.asm) provides vector/trig math (`rlGetVectorLength`, `rlMultiplyByNegSin`, `rlMultiplyByNegSinMode7`, `aMapMagicSineTable`), palette darkening/tinting/restoring (`rlDarkenBGPaletteRange`, `rlDarkenSNESColor`, `rlDarkenMapMagicColors`, `rlFadeBackMapMagicColors`, `rlSaveMapMagicColors`, `rsTintMapMagicColorsRed`/`Blue`/`Green`, `rsFadeMapMagicPalette14ToWhite`/`Black`), background shake (`rsShakeMapMagicBackground`), DMA list queueing (`rlQueueMapMagicDMAList`), and the bank `$87` BG2 HDMA ripple effect (`procUnknown87C70A`, `rlProcMapMagicRippleInit`..`rsHDMAMapMagicRippleLargeInit`). All effect steps, shared HDMA/window builders, and cutscene sequence helpers in [map_magic_effects.asm](../../engine/battle/map_magic_effects.asm) are now named and carry a note naming their effect.

**Active sprite scripts.** The effects draw with active sprites, whose scripts are now written with the `ACTIVE_SPRITE_*` macros in `include/ActiveSpriteEngine.h` (`tools/sprite_scripts.py` decodes one from the ROM). Command `$17` calls a routine; many of the routines it calls read a script position after the call and jump there or skip it (they load the word at `Y` into `Y`).

## The other animations

- **Village raid** (`procVillageRaid`, from `rlStartVillageRaidAnimation`): queues the unit's sprites and effect graphics (`rlMapActionQueueEffectGraphics`), loads the fire background and sprite (`rlVillageRaidCreateFireSprite`), and triggers `rlStartMapMagicVillageFire` (`rlVillageRaidStartFireEffect`).
- **Return home** (`procReturnHome`, from `rlStartReturnHomeAnimation`): queues the unit's sprites, plays the Return magic effect (`rlReturnHomeStartMagicRound`, `rlProcWaitMapMagicDoneCycle`), and restores OAM/map animations (`rlEndReturnHomeSprites`).
- **Give gold** (`procGiveGold`, from `rlStartGiveGoldAnimation`): shows both units, uploads coin graphics (`rlGiveGoldUploadCoinGraphics`) and plays `aGiveGoldCoinSpriteData` (`rlGiveGoldCreateCoinSprite`), then runs the experience and level-up steps.
- **Holy weapon shine** (`procHolyWeaponShine`, from `rlStartHolyWeaponShine`): sets up the unit (`rlSetHolyWeaponShineUnit`), plays `rlMapBattleStartSkillEffect`, and signals completion (`rlSignalMapAnimationDone`).
- **Rewards without a battle** (`procMapActionRewards`, from `rlStartMapActionRewards`): experience, level up, gold, item, using the battle's steps, then restores graphics (`rlProcMapActionRewardsRestoreGraphics`, `rlProcMapActionRewardsEnd`).
- **Scripted critical** (`procScriptedCritStart`, `procScriptedCritEffect`): before a battle with a scripted critical (`rlStartCombatAnimation`), the critical's effect plays on the map (`aMapBattleScriptedCritSprites`).
- **Warp away / warp in** (`procWarpAway`, `procWarpIn`) and **the Belhalla mages** (`procBelhallaAttackingMage`, `procBelhallaMagesBlast`), from event ASMCs.

## Sprites

Each side gets a main sprite and, for the mounted and flying classes (byte `$30` of their row in `aBattleMapSpriteData`, `wSideTallSprite`), an aux sprite. Their definitions are built in RAM (`rlInitMapBattleSpriteInfos`, `$7EFC88`) because their scripts are in the decompressed sprite data (bank `$7E`). A class's row holds, per facing (0-7), a long pointer to its sprite data and one to its graphics (`rlQueueMapBattleSpriteGraphics`). The sprite data has one block per weapon type: a frame table, then script pairs (main, aux): +6 wait for a round, +`$0A` dodge, +`$0E` strike, +`$12` strike at range (with the magic effect). `rsGetMapBattleRoundScripts` picks a pair per round; sprite commands `$09`/`$0A` jump to it. A strike is a handshake: the striker's script runs `$04` (`wMapBattleStrikeSignal`, the hit sound), the struck side recoils and flashes, `$05` waits until the signal is cleared and sets `wMapBattleStrikeDone`, `$07` waits for that, `$0D` ends the round. The ballista uses `$1B` (fire), `$1A` (bolt progress) and `$1C` (landed); the bolt flies 16 frames along an arc chosen by distance (`aBallistaBoltArcs`). The commands' macros are in `include/ActiveSpriteEngine.h`; `tools/sprite_scripts.py` writes them.

Other pieces: the weapon icon over each unit (`rlUploadMapBattleWeaponIcon`), the shadow, the level up banner and stat gains (`g4bppMapBattleLevelUpText`), the defeat blink (`rlMapBattleDefeatBlink`), the skill activation effect (`rlMapBattleStartSkillEffect`: BG palette 13 by skill, sound `$3D`), a status sword's staff effect after its strike (`rlMapBattleStartStatusSwordEffect`). A battle on the castle screen (`wBattleType` 3) uses the courtyard sprite positions and hides the units with `DeploymentStateHiddenInCastle`. Staves that change no HP (Fortify, Return, Warp, Rescue, Restore, Silence, Sleep, Berserk, Thief) open no HP windows (`rlCheckMapBattleNoHPChange`).
