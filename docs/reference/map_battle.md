# The map battle

When battle animations are off, a fight is played on the map itself: the two units' small battle sprites face each other, swing, and the HP windows count down. The same sprites and helpers also show a few map actions and scripted scenes. All of it is bank `$9E`. Per-routine details: [routines/battle.md](routines/battle.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## Files

| File | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| [map_battle.asm](../../engine/battle/map_battle.asm) | `procMapBattle` and everything it uses: the combatants' sprites (`aBattleMapSpriteData`), the rounds, ballista bolts, quotes, the level up banner and stat gains, the gold and item popups, the clean-up; the scripted critical's effect |
| [map_action_animations.asm](../../engine/battle/map_action_animations.asm) | village raid, return home, holy weapon shine, give gold, rewards of an action without a battle |
| [map_battle_hud.asm](../../engine/battle/map_battle_hud.asm) | the HP and experience windows and the text box (bank `$85`), and `procMapExperienceWindowEffect` |
| [cutscenes.asm](../../engine/events/cutscenes.asm) | the warp away/in animations, the Belhalla mages, and the starters for scripted map battles that events run |

The map magic effects are in [map_battle_magic.asm](../../engine/battle/map_battle_magic.asm) (bank `$9F`, not yet sorted).

Data: `aMapAction` (`$7F454A`) and `wMapBattleMode` (`$7F4C87`) in [include/WRAM.h](../../include/WRAM.h); the rounds come from `aBattleRoundsData`, written by the battle calculation.

## Starting it

`rlStartMapBattleAnimation` creates `procMapBattle` in the first free proc slot. `wMapBattleMode` says why:

- **0, a battle**: `rlStartMapBattle` (bank `$81`) when the animations are off.
- **1, the opening's demo battles**: `rlASMCStartDemoMapBattle`, run by the opening's event script.
- **2, a scripted battle from an event**: `rlStartScriptedMapBattleAnimation` (two units, e.g. Arvis attacking Sigurd) and `rlStartSoloMapBattleAnimation` (one unit, Silvia's dance in chapter 4). These fill the battle action structs themselves.

Only mode 0 changes HP (`rlApplyMapBattleRoundHP`).

## How a battle runs

The proc's script, `aProcMapBattleCode`, is the battle's outline. Most steps are a routine that sets proc header bit `$0002` when it has something to do and bit `$0004` when it is done, and the script waits on those bits.

1. **Set up** (`rlProcMapBattleInit`, `rlInitMapBattleCombatants`): pauses the map's sprite and tile animations, and fills `aMapAction` from the two action structs: each side's battle sprite class (`rlGetMapBattleClassSprite`: some classes have a female sprite, and Seliph as a Knight Lord has his own), weapon type, tile position and facing (`rlGetMapBattleFacing`).
2. **Camera** (`rlMapBattleSetCameraTarget`, `rlMapBattleMoveCamera`): scrolls the map until both units are on screen.
3. **Sprites**: the attacker's then the defender's battle sprites appear (`rlShowMapBattleAttacker`, `rlShowMapBattleDefender`) over their hidden map sprites.
4. **Battle quote** (`rlMapBattleStartBattleQuote`): when `wBattleQuotePointer` is set, a dialogue box; afterwards the sprite tiles it covered are decompressed and uploaded again.
5. **HP windows** (`rlProcMapBattleOpenHPWindows`).
6. **Rounds** (`rlMapBattleNextRound` and the steps after it), until the entry `$FFFF` in `aBattleRoundsData`: each round picks its sprite scripts by side and by its hit, miss and critical bits (`rsGetMapBattleRoundScripts`), may fire a ballista bolt (`procMapBattleBallistaBolt`, when a side is one of the four ballistician classes) or a map magic effect (bank `$9F`), and applies the HP change.
7. **Death quote** (`rlMapBattleStartDeathQuote`).
8. **Experience** (`rlMapBattleOpenExperienceWindow`), then **level up** (`rlMapBattleStartLevelUpBanner`, `rlMapBattleStatGains`, with `rlPlayLevelUpJingle` and `rlPlayStatGainSound`).
9. **Popups**, each with the music lowered and a jingle: gold obtained (ゴールド　てにいれた！), gold stolen (ゴールド　ぬすまれた), an item obtained (を　てにいれた！), a weapon broken (は　こわれてしまった).
10. **End** (`rlProcMapBattleEnd`, `rlEndMapBattleRestoreMap`): the camera returns to the cursor, the windows close, the palettes and the units' map sprites come back, and canto is checked.

## The other animations

- **Village raid** (`procVillageRaid`, from `rlUnitRaidVillage`).
- **Return home** (`procReturnHome`, from `rlUnitReturnToHomeCastle`).
- **Give gold** (`procGiveGold`, from `rsMapActionGiveGold` and `rlCastleGiveMoney`).
- **Holy weapon shine** (`procHolyWeaponShine`, from the `...ObtainShine` ASMCs).
- **Rewards without a battle** (`procMapActionRewards`, from `rsRelocateRemoveHelpedUnit` and an ASMC): experience, level up, gold, item, using the battle's steps.
- **Scripted critical** (`procScriptedCritStart`, `procScriptedCritEffect`): before a battle with a scripted critical (`rlUnknown84DE36`), the critical's effect plays on the map (`aMapBattleScriptedCritSprites`).
- **Warp away / warp in** (`procWarpAway`, `procWarpIn`) and **the Belhalla mages** (`procBelhallaAttackingMage`, `procBelhallaMagesBlast`), from event ASMCs.

## Depends on
