# The battle scene

The battle scene is the full-screen battle that plays when the animations are on: two combatants on a background of their terrains, HP at the bottom, the strikes, then the experience bar, level up and any messages. It is game states `$18`/`$19`, and its code fills banks `$95` and `$96`, with data in `$97`-`$99` and `$E0`.

The map's own battle, with the animations off, is a different system ([map_battle.md](map_battle.md)). The title screen borrows the scene's engines for its effects ([title.md](title.md)).

Per-routine details: [routines/battle_scene.md](routines/battle_scene.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

The scene's RAM is in [WRAM.h](../../include/WRAM.h): the animation slots at `$0AF3` (`bBattleAnimationStateFlags`...), the round state at `$0ADD`-`$0AE8`, the combatants at `$0D44`-`$0D69`, and variables from `$7FE09E` to `$7FFC59`. The map's text buffer and unit buffers share `$7FE000`-`$7FFFFF`, so `tools/name_ram.py` writes the battle scene's names only in `engine/battle_scene/` and `engine/title/`.

## How it is reached

`rlStartBattleScene` (bank `$81`) runs when a battle has the animations on. It stops the music and either fades out through `procMainLoopChangeFade` or, for an ordinary battle (`wBattleType` 0), plays the window wipe (`aBattleTransitionHDMAInfo`). Either way it ends in `rsOpenBattleScene`. Bank `$84` and the castle's class change also call it, and the demo battles open the scene directly.

`rsOpenBattleScene` resets the engines and clears the four animation slots (`rlBattleSceneClearCombatants`). It runs `rlBattleSceneInit`, installs five IRQ entries, and sets game state `$18` with `rsRunBattleSceneVBlank` and `rsRunBattleSceneMainLoop`.

`rlBattleSceneInit`:

1. sets the PPU (`rsBattleSceneSetPPURegisters`) and the scene's flags (`rlBattleSceneSetFlags`);
2. copies the two combatants from `aActionStructUnit1`/`2` into `aBattleSceneCombatant1`/`2`: class, character, weapon, HP, maximum HP, gender, plus the terrains and the distance (`rsBattleSceneCopyCombatants`), and the experience (`rlBattleSceneCopyExperience`);
3. resets the scene's state (`rsBattleSceneResetState`): `lBattleSceneRoundPointer` points at the first entry of `aBattleRoundsData`, and `bBattleSceneStep` is 3;
4. loads the background and foreground for the two terrains (`rsBattleSceneLoadBackground`), and each combatant's weapon, extra tiles, sheet, palette and character piece (`rsBattleSceneLoadCombatantGraphics`);
5. places the combatants facing each other (`rsBattleScenePlaceCombatants`) and sets the camera (`wBattleSceneCameraX`).

## The frame

Game state `$18` (`rlBattleSceneSetup`) blanks the screen once. Game state `$19` (`rlBattleSceneStep`) runs `rsBattleSceneRunFrame` every frame until it returns carry set. That happens when the fade out is over (`bBattleSceneFade` `$40` with brightness 0). The step then leaves by `wBattleSceneFlags` and `wBattleType`: to the map (`wMainGameMode` 2, or 3 after Julius), back to the castle after the arena, to the castle screen (types 2 and 3), or to game state 0.

`rsBattleSceneRunFrame` runs, in this order:

1. **Before the rounds.** The intro steps (`rlBattleSceneRunIntroStep`), then `rlBattleSceneCheckIntroDone` (it sets `BattleSceneRoundMayRun`), the brightness table, the fade.
2. **The rounds.** While `BattleSceneRoundMayRun` is set: the move queue (`rlBattleAnimationRunMoveQueue`) and `rlBattleSceneRunRounds`.
3. **The combatants' motion.** X, gravity, jump and Y for slots `$00` and `$43`; the effect slots `$86` and `$C9`.
4. **The rest of the frame.** The sequences (`rlBattleAnimationUpdateAll`), the battle scene script (`rlRunBattleSceneScript`), the effects, the camera and the HP bars (`rsBattleSceneUpdateHP1`/`2`).
5. **VBlank.** It waits for VBlank (`$7FE1CF`), then builds the HDMA tables.
6. **The end.** The arena's retreat button, the arena's next bout (`rlBattleSceneCheckArenaBout`) and the end steps (`rlBattleSceneRunEndStep`).

## The rounds

The battle calculation (`rlActionStructBuildBattleRounds`, bank `$84`) leaves one word per strike in `aBattleRoundsData`:

| Entry | Meaning |
|---|---|
| low byte | `bBattleRoundFlags`: bit 0 the side that strikes (0 combatant 1), `$02` a staff, `$04` critical, `$08` miss, `$10` hit kind 3 (the Julius strike; original notes: "vs Julius, arena"), `$20` ranged, `$40` magic |
| high byte | the damage (`bBattleSceneRoundDamage`) |
| `$xxFE` | a skill activates: the high byte is its `SkillID` |
| `$xxFD` | skipped |
| `$FEFF` | the end of an arena bout: fight again unless the player pressed B |
| `$FFFF` | the end |

`rlBattleSceneRunRounds` waits until nothing is moving (`rsBattleSceneCheckBusy`). Then `rsBattleSceneStartNextRound` reads one entry:

- **Holy weapon.** At the first round of a combatant with a holy weapon (`rsBattleSceneCheckHolyWeapon`: Tyrfing, Gungnir... Valkyrie Staff), it plays script `$43` and sound `$90` (`rlBattleScenePlayHolyWeaponSound`) before anything else.
- **Sides.** It sets `wBattleAnimationAttackerOffset`/`DefenderOffset` and the roles (`rsBattleSceneSetRoles`). It pans the camera when the attacker is off the screen's middle (`rlBattleSceneCheckCombatant1OnScreen`).
- **The action.** It queues the attacker's action by the attack style in its `AnimStyle` (the low nibble): `rlBattleSceneQueueStaffAction`, `rlBattleSceneQueueAttack`, `rlBattleSceneQueueCriticalAttack`, or `rlBattleSceneQueueSpecialCriticalAttack` (with bit `$10`). A far attacker may first close in (`rlBattleSceneQueueApproach`).
- **Skills.** A skill entry shows its banner (`rlBattleSceneShowSkill`: Astra, Luna, Sol, Holy, Darkness, Pavise). Each banner has its colors and a script, `$36`-`$3A` and `$3F`.

The strike lands when the attacker's sequence reaches its command (`ANIM_CMD_F1`) or script command `$FFFA`. Either way it calls `rlBattleSceneApplyStrike`:

- the defender's HP starts falling by the damage at a point a frame (`rsBattleSceneStartHPLoss1`/`2`);
- the defender flashes for 25 frames and is knocked back (`rlBattleSceneSetKnockback`);
- `rlBattleScenePlayHitSound` plays `$91`-`$94` by `bBattleSceneHitKind`, `$95` for no damage, panned to the defender, with `$50` added for a critical;
- with EarthSword or Sol, the attacker regains the damage (`rlBattleSceneStartDrain`).

Script command `$FFFB` heals instead (`rlBattleSceneStartHeal`).

HP is kept as 8.8 in each combatant's record (`HP`). `rsBattleSceneUpdateHP1`/`2` move it by `HPSpeed` toward `HPTarget`, play sound `$0D` for each whole point, and redraw the number and bar (`rsBattleSceneDrawHP1`/`2`).

**The arena.** In an arena battle (`wBattleType` 1), B sets `BattleSceneRoundArenaRetreat` (`rlBattleSceneReadArenaRetreat`). At `$FEFF`, the scene fights another bout unless the player retreated: `rlBattleSceneRestartRounds` points back at the first round.

## The end steps

After the rounds, `rlBattleSceneRunEndStep` takes the 21 routines of `aBattleSceneEndSteps`, last to first, one at a time whenever nothing else is running. Most start a battle scene script:

1. `rlBattleSceneShowBG3`;
2. scripts `$33` and `$35` in battle type 2, and `$34` and `$31` when bit 7 of `bBattleSceneStrikeFlags` is set (not with Julius defending);
3. the death quote (`rlBattleSceneShowDeathQuote`, `wDeathQuotePointer`);
4. the experience bar (script 0, `rlBattleSceneShowExperience`);
5. a level up: the lead-in (`rlBattleScenePrepareLevelUp`), the banner (`$3B`), the stat gains (script 1, `rlBattleSceneShowLevelUp`);
6. the message window (`$2A`) and each message, with sound `$92` or `$93`:
   - an item obtained (`$2C`),
   - gold obtained or stolen (`$2F`),
   - a broken weapon (`$44`),

   each waiting for a button (`$30`);
7. Julius's scripts when the battle ends on him (`$4A`, `$4B`/`$3D`, `$4C`);
8. the fade out (`rlBattleSceneStartFadeOut`).

The intro has three steps (`aBattleSceneIntroSteps`): fade in, the opening, and the wait for it to end (`rlBattleSceneWaitForIntro`, which sets the end step count).

**Julius.** A strike of hit kind 3 (round bit `$10`) by combatant 1 on `JuliusChFinal` sets `BattleSceneFlagToMapMode3`, slows the fade and fades the music (`rlBattleScenePlayHitSound`). The death quote is then shown by `rlBattleSceneShowJuliusQuote` instead, and the scene returns to the map in mode 3.

## Animation slots

Four slots of `$43` bytes from `$0AF3`: the two combatants (`X` = 0 and `$43`) and two effect slots (`$86`, `$C9`).

- **Sequences.** Each slot plays a sequence script (`rsBattleAnimationRunScript`): poses with their frames, and the commands `$E8`-`$FF` in [BattleAnimation.h](../../include/BattleAnimation.h). `rsBattleAnimationReadStep` reads each step word minus 1, so command `$FF` is compared as `$FE`.
- **Actions.** An action (`rlBattleAnimationQueueAction`) is a byte of the class's move program: a move list or a single sequence. Bits 0-1 of `$0AF5,x` pick its variant (`ANIM_CMD_ED`).
- **Movement.** The move lists' steps run the move routines in [animation_moves.asm](../../engine/battle_scene/animation_moves.asm).

## Depends on

- **The battle calculation** (bank `$84`, [engine/battle/battle.asm](../../engine/battle/battle.asm)): the action structs and `aBattleRoundsData`.
- **The core:** the HDMA engine, the IRQ engine (`rlUnknown82E0C6`), the decompressor, DMA, sound (`rlPlaySoundEffect2`, `rlGetSoundPanFromXPixel`), the dialogue engine for the messages.
- **Used by** the title screen (its effects and `wBattleSceneFrame`) and the demo battles.

## Open questions

- **Battle type 2.** What it is: the scene clears the quotes and drops, has no foreground, and uses scripts `$33`/`$35`. It leaves to the castle screen like type 3.
- **`BattleSceneFlagSolo`** (`aActionStructUnit1.Unknown` = 8, both combatants at the center, action 1): which action sets it.
- **Unused flags.** Bits `$0100`/`$0200` of `wBattleSceneFlags` (weapons of `aUnknown998F02`) are set, but no reader was found.
- **`bBattleSceneRanged`.** It is never stored: `rsBattleSceneStartNextRound` loads the ranged bit into A and branches past the store.
- **A wrong label.** `rsBattleSceneLoadExtraTiles` loads a long pointer written as `aChapter05MapEvents + 326` for extra tiles ID 0. The label is a chance match and the target is probably blank tiles; it needs a proper label.
- **`rsBattleScenePlaceCombatants`** holds two unreachable `.byte` runs after `bra` (dead code).
- **Unknown routines.** The visual effects (`effects.asm`, `screen.asm`) and most of the slot engine and HUD are still `Unknown`. So are the palette effect script format (`rlBattleSceneStartPaletteEffect`, scripts at `$97/A9A8` and in bank `$99`) and the window effect data (`$97/F64E`, `$97/A7C7`).
