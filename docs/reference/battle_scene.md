# The battle scene

The battle scene is the full-screen battle that plays when the animations are on: two combatants on a background of their terrains, HP at the bottom, the strikes, then the experience bar, level up and any messages. It is game states `$18`/`$19`, and its code fills banks `$95` and `$96`, with data in `$97`-`$99` and `$E0`.

The map's own battle, with the animations off, is a different system ([map_battle.md](map_battle.md)). The title screen borrows the scene's engines for its effects ([title.md](title.md)).

Per-routine details: [routines/battle_scene.md](routines/battle_scene.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

The map's text buffer and unit buffers share `$7FE000`-`$7FFFFF`, so `tools/name_ram.py` writes the battle scene's names only in `engine/battle_scene/` and `engine/title/`.

## How it is reached

`rlStartBattleScene` (bank `$81`) runs when a battle has the animations on. It stops the music and either fades out through `procMainLoopChangeFade` or, for an ordinary battle (`wBattleType` 0), plays the window wipe (`aBattleTransitionHDMAInfo`). Either way it ends in `rsOpenBattleScene`. Bank `$84` and the castle's class change also call it, and the demo battles open the scene directly.

`rsOpenBattleScene` resets the engines and clears the four animation slots (`rlBattleSceneClearCombatants`). It runs `rlBattleSceneInit`, installs five IRQ entries, and sets game state `$18` with `rsRunBattleSceneVBlank` and `rsRunBattleSceneMainLoop`.

`rlBattleSceneInit`:

1. sets the PPU (`rsBattleSceneSetPPURegisters`) and the scene's flags (`rlBattleSceneSetFlags`);
2. copies the two combatants from `aActionStructUnit1`/`2` into `aBattleSceneCombatant1`/`2`: class, character, weapon, HP, maximum HP, gender, plus the terrains and the distance (`rsBattleSceneCopyCombatants`), and the experience (`rlBattleSceneCopyExperience`);
3. resets the scene's state (`rsBattleSceneResetState`): `lBattleSceneRoundPointer` points at the first entry of `aBattleRoundsData`, and `bBattleSceneStep` is 3;
4. loads the background and foreground for the two terrains (`rsBattleSceneLoadBackground`), and each combatant's weapon, extra tiles, sheet, palette and character piece (`rsBattleSceneLoadCombatantGraphics`);
5. places the combatants facing each other (`rsBattleScenePlaceCombatants`) and sets the camera (`wBattleSceneCameraX`).

**Placing the combatants.** They start apart by `rlBattleSceneGetStartGap`: twice byte 6 of the combatant's body record, `$C8` for a magic sword at range, `$E0` for a staff used from distance 2. Then the first striker's weapon (`rlBattleSceneGetFirstStriker`) can override the placement (`rlBattleSceneApplyPlacement`, `aBattleSceneWeaponPlacements`):

| Mode | Weapons | Placement |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Meteor, Bolting, Blizzard, Fenrir, the ballistae; Physic, Restore, Silence, Sleep and Berserk Staves at distance 3 or more | the striker alone at the center, the other hidden |
| 1 | Fortify | both at the center (`BattleSceneFlagSolo2`) |
| 2 | Rescue, Valkyrie | the other hidden |
| 3 | battle type 2, any weapon | both at the center on the ground, the other hidden |
| 4 | the magic and status swords | nothing |

A hidden combatant has bit 6 of `bBattleAnimationCombatantFlags` set: neither it nor its shadow is drawn. Script routines show and hide either side (`rlBattleSceneScriptHideDefender`...).

**The dance.** Action struct field `$22` = 8 is the dance (battle.asm's `rsActionStructDance` refreshes every unit in range). The scene sets `BattleSceneFlagDance` and `BattleSceneFlagSolo2`, puts both combatants at the center, queues action 1 (`rlBattleSceneQueueDanceAction`), and gives a Dancer (class `$32`) the dance's animation IDs (`rlBattleAnimationCheckDance`).

**Animations.** `rlBattleSceneSetAnimStyles` sets each combatant's `AnimStyle` from its weapon (`aItemAnimationCategories`): the high nibble is the weapon category of the animation lookup, the low nibble the attack style (`AttackStyleSword`, `Lance`, `Axe`, `Ranged`, `Magic`, `Staff`). A javelin or hand axe becomes `AttackStyleRanged` at distance 2 or more, else the plain lance or axe. `rlBattleSceneLookUpAnimations` then takes the four animation IDs from `aBattleAnimationLookup` (`rlBattleAnimationLookUp`). A few characters get three colors of their own over sprite colors 12-14 (`rlBattleSceneLoadCharacterColors`).

## The frame

Game state `$18` (`rlBattleSceneSetup`) blanks the screen once. Game state `$19` (`rlBattleSceneStep`) runs `rsBattleSceneRunFrame` every frame until it returns carry set. That happens when the fade out is over (`bBattleSceneFade` `$40` with brightness 0). The step then leaves by `wBattleSceneFlags` and `wBattleType`: to the map (`wMainGameMode` 2, or 3 after Julius), back to the castle after the arena, to the castle screen (types 2 and 3), or to game state 0.

`rsBattleSceneRunFrame` runs, in this order:

1. **Before the rounds.** The intro steps (`rlBattleSceneRunIntroStep`), then `rlBattleSceneCheckIntroDone` (it sets `BattleSceneRoundMayRun`), the brightness table, the fade.
2. **The rounds.** While `BattleSceneRoundMayRun` is set: the move queue (`rlBattleAnimationRunMoveQueue`) and `rlBattleSceneRunRounds`.
3. **The combatants' motion.** X, gravity, jump and Y for slots `$00` and `$43`; the effect slots `$86` and `$C9`.
4. **The rest of the frame.** The sequences (`rlBattleAnimationUpdateAll`), the battle scene script (`rlRunBattleSceneScript`), the effects, the camera and the HP bars (`rsBattleSceneUpdateHP1`/`2`).
5. **VBlank.** It waits for VBlank (`$7FE1CF`), then builds the HDMA tables.
6. **The end.** The arena's retreat button, the arena's next bout (`rlBattleSceneCheckArenaBout`) and the end steps (`rlBattleSceneRunEndStep`).

## The rounds

The battle calculation (`rlActionStructBuildBattleRounds`, bank `$84`) leaves one word per strike in `aBattleRoundsData`:

| Entry | Meaning |
|---|---|
| low byte | `bBattleRoundFlags`: bit 0 the side that strikes (0 combatant 1), `$02` a staff, `$04` critical, `$08` miss, `$10` fatal (`BattleRoundFlagFatal`: the strike defeats the defender), `$20` ranged, `$40` magic |
| high byte | the damage (`bBattleSceneRoundDamage`) |
| `$xxFE` | a skill activates: the high byte is its `SkillID` |
| `$xxFD` | skipped |
| `$FEFF` | the end of an arena bout: fight again unless the player pressed B |
| `$FFFF` | the end |

`rlBattleSceneRunRounds` waits until nothing is moving (`rsBattleSceneCheckBusy`). Then `rsBattleSceneStartNextRound` reads one entry:

- **Holy weapon.** At the first round of a combatant with a holy weapon (`rsBattleSceneCheckHolyWeapon`: Tyrfing, Gungnir... Valkyrie Staff), it plays script `$43` and sound `$90` (`rlBattleScenePlayHolyWeaponSound`) before anything else.
- **Sides.** It sets `wBattleAnimationAttackerOffset`/`DefenderOffset` and the roles (`rsBattleSceneSetRoles`). It pans the camera when the attacker is off the screen's middle (`rlBattleSceneCheckCombatant1OnScreen`).
- **The action.** It queues the attacker's action by the attack style in its `AnimStyle` (the low nibble): `rlBattleSceneQueueStaffAction`, `rlBattleSceneQueueAttack`, `rlBattleSceneQueueCriticalAttack`, or `rlBattleSceneQueueSpecialCriticalAttack` (a fatal critical). The action numbers come from `aBattleSceneAttackActions` and the tables after it, one byte per attack style, plus the combatant's variant (bits 0-1 of `bBattleAnimationCombatantFlags`). A far attacker may first close in (`rlBattleSceneQueueApproach`).
- **Magic swords.** For a magic round by a sword wielder, `rlBattleSceneCheckMagicSword` decides first. The Berserk, Silence and Sleep Swords start their weapon script at once. Any other sword striking from range sets bit 2 of `bBattleAnimationCombatantFlags`, and `rlBattleSceneQueueMagicSwordAttack` queues a swing (`aBattleSceneMagicSwordSwingActions`) and then the spell (`aBattleSceneMagicSwordSpellActions`).
- **Weapon scripts.** Tomes, staves and the magic swords each name a battle scene script in `aBattleSceneWeaponScripts`; the four ranged staves name another at distance 3 or more. Sequence command `$F0` starts it (`rlBattleSceneGetWeaponScript`).
- **The last round.** Before reading an entry, the reader looks at the one before it: after a fatal strike it sets bit 7 of `bBattleSceneStrikeFlags` and reads no more.
- **Skills.** A skill entry shows its banner (`rlBattleSceneShowSkill`: Astra, Luna, Sol, Holy, Darkness, Pavise). Each banner has its colors and a script, `$36`-`$3A` and `$3F`.

The strike lands when the attacker's sequence reaches its command (`ANIM_CMD_F1`) or script command `$FFFA`. Either way it calls `rlBattleSceneApplyStrike`:

- the defender's HP starts falling by the damage at a point a frame (`rsBattleSceneStartHPLoss1`/`2`);
- the defender flashes for 25 frames and is knocked back (`rlBattleSceneSetKnockback`);
- `rlBattleScenePlayHitSound` plays `$91`-`$94` by `bBattleSceneHitKind`, `$95` for no damage, panned to the defender, with `$50` added for a critical;
- with EarthSword or Sol, the attacker regains the damage (`rlBattleSceneStartDrain`).

Script command `$FFFB` heals instead (`rlBattleSceneStartHeal`).

HP is kept as 8.8 in each combatant's record (`HP`). `rsBattleSceneUpdateHP1`/`2` move it by `HPSpeed` toward `HPTarget`, play sound `$0D` for each whole point, and redraw the number and bar (`rsBattleSceneDrawHP1`/`2`).

**The arena.** In an arena battle (`wBattleType` 1), B sets `BattleSceneRoundArenaRetreat` (`rlBattleSceneReadArenaRetreat`). At `$FEFF`, the scene fights another bout unless the player retreated: `rlBattleSceneRestartRounds` points back at the first round.

## The end steps

After the rounds, `rlBattleSceneRunEndStep` takes the 21 routines of `aBattleSceneEndSteps`, last to first, one at a time whenever nothing else is running. Most start a battle scene script:

1. `rlBattleSceneShowBG3`;
2. scripts `$33` and `$35` in battle type 2, and `$34` and `$31` after a fatal strike (bit 7 of `bBattleSceneStrikeFlags`; not with Julius defending), with the defeat cry: sound `$89`-`$8B` by the defeated unit's voice (`rlBattleSceneEndStepDefeatCry`, `rlGetDefeatedUnitVoice`: 1 + its gender, or 0 for none);
3. the death quote (`rlBattleSceneShowDeathQuote`, `wDeathQuotePointer`);
4. the experience bar (script 0, `rlBattleSceneShowExperience`);
5. a level up: the lead-in (`rlBattleScenePrepareLevelUp`), the banner (`$3B`), the stat gains (script 1, `rlBattleSceneShowLevelUp`);
6. the message window (`$2A`) and each message, with sound `$92` or `$93`:
   - an item obtained (`$2C`),
   - gold obtained or stolen (`$2F`),
   - a broken weapon (`$44`),

   each waiting for a button (`$30`). Script routines `$1C`-`$23` run the window: set up the dialogue engine for it (`rsBattleSceneSetUpMessageDialogue`), start the text at `lBattleSceneDialoguePointer`, and every frame run the dialogue, upload its tilemap and draw the button arrow until the delay at `$7FE4D9` has run out and the text is done (`rlBattleSceneScriptRunMessage`);
7. Julius's scripts when the battle ends on him (`$4A`, `$4B`/`$3D`, `$4C`);
8. the fade out (`rlBattleSceneStartFadeOut`).

The intro has three steps (`aBattleSceneIntroSteps`): fade in, the opening, and the wait for it to end (`rlBattleSceneWaitForIntro`, which sets the end step count).

**Julius.** A fatal strike (round bit `$10`, hit kind 3) by combatant 1 on `JuliusChFinal` sets `BattleSceneFlagToMapMode3`, slows the fade and fades the music (`rlBattleScenePlayHitSound`). The death quote is then shown by `rlBattleSceneShowJuliusQuote` instead, and the scene returns to the map in mode 3.

## The HUD

`rlBattleSceneLoadHUD` builds the bottom of the screen once, in `rlBattleSceneInit`'s last steps: the HUD's tiles, the window tilemap (`$7FC800`, VRAM `$D000`) and the HUD tilemap (`$7FD0C0`, VRAM `$D8C0`). Into the HUD tilemap go:

- **the stats**: ten numbers listed in `aBattleSceneHUDStats`, each combatant's `CurrentHP`, `Level`, `BattleAdjustedHit`, `BattleMight` and `BattleDefense` from its action struct (`rsBattleSceneDrawStats`; 255 shows as two dashes, `rlBattleSceneDrawStat`);
- **HP** and its bar (`rsBattleSceneDrawHP1`/`2`, `rlBattleSceneDrawHPBar`); every frame `rlBattleSceneQueueHPUpload` sends those rows again;
- **colors by faction**: `rlBattleSceneSetHUDColors` takes each combatant's HUD color (`rlGetFactionBattleHUDColorID`, `aBattleSceneHUDColors`) and `rsBattleSceneLoadBanners` its faction banner (`aBattleBannerTable`);
- **names**: `rlBattleSceneLoadNames` draws each name and weapon name with the font into 2bpp tiles at `$7EAC88` (8 characters, two tile rows each; combatant 2's right-aligned, combatant 1's left-aligned) and sends them to VRAM `$F800`.

A combatant with no character (0) gets its half blanked (`rlBattleSceneBlankMissingCombatant`). In battle type 2 the HUD's palettes move (`rlBattleSceneRecolorHUDForType2`) and the scene's colors dim (`rlBattleSceneDimPalettesForType2`).

**The gauge.** One set of variables at `$7FED4B` (`wBattleSceneGaugeValue`...) draws a number with an optional bar and counts it toward a target at an 8.8 speed:

- the experience bar: script routine 13 (`rlBattleSceneStartExperienceGauge`) sets it from `wBattleSceneExperience` to that plus `wBattleSceneExperienceGained`; routine 1 plays sound `$0B` and fills it every frame (`rlBattleSceneRunExperienceFill`), wrapping past 100, and plays `$0C` when it stops;
- the level up window: routine 2 draws the nine gauges of `aBattleSceneLevelUpGauges` (the level and the eight stats); `rlBattleSceneStartStatGauge` then raises one stat by its gain (`aBattleSceneLevelUpStatGauges`) and `rlBattleSceneRunStatGauge` fills it. HP's bar holds 80 points, the other stats' 30.

The gauge draws into the window tilemap and queues the changed rows with `rlBattleSceneQueueUpload`.

**The shake.** A critical (bit `$40` of `bBattleSceneStrikeFlags`) shakes the HUD (`rlBattleSceneStartHUDShake`): `$7FFC73`-`$7FFC76` are the BG2/BG3 scroll that the IRQ above the HUD writes, moved by `aBattleSceneHUDShakeOffsets` until `$8080`.

**Uploads.** `rlBattleSceneQueueUpload` queues up to 16 copies to VRAM (`aBattleSceneUploads`, `structBattleSceneUpload`); `rlBattleSceneRunUploads` sends them in VBlank, at most `$0C00` bytes a frame, the rest the next frame. Bigger copies go through `rlBattleSceneStartLargeUpload`, which feeds the queue a part each frame.

## Palette effects

Ten slots (`aBattleScenePaletteSlots` at `$7FFD9A`, `structBattleScenePaletteSlot`) change colors of `aBGPaletteBuffer` every frame (`rlBattleSceneRunPaletteEffects`, by type). `rlBattleSceneStartPaletteEffect` fills one from an entry: slot, type, first color, color count, then by type a long pointer, a byte, both, or nothing (`PALETTE_EFFECT`, `_VALUE`, `_VALUE_DATA`, `_NO_DATA`). Script command 11 takes its entry from `aBattleScenePaletteEffects` (191 offsets in bank `$97`); the title screen and the map battle pass their own. The colors are in `data.asm` (bank `$9B`) and bank `$E0`. `tools/palette_effects.py` wrote the 204 entries. The types (`aBattleScenePaletteEffectTypes`):

| Type | Does |
|---|---|
| 0 | frames of colors: each frame an optional `$80 \| frames` delay byte and the colors; `$FE` loops, `$FF` ends |
| 1, 2 | scroll the colors down (up) by one and put the next color of the data at the end (start) |
| 3, 4 | rotate the colors down (up) every Value frames |
| 5, 6 | fade a step at a time to black (white) |
| 7 | fade a step at a time toward the colors in the data |
| 8, 9 | set black (white) |
| 10 | set the colors in the data |
| 11 | restore the saved palette at `$7FFE00` |
| 12 | as 1, the new color brightened by `wBattleScenePaletteBrighten` steps |

A slot that ends with bit 6 of its type set counts down the script's wait (`bBattleSceneScriptWaitCount`).

## Animation slots

Four slots of `$43` bytes from `$0AF3`: the two combatants (`X` = 0 and `$43`) and their effect slots (`$86` for combatant 1, `$C9` for combatant 2: arrows, thrown weapons, magic).

- **Sequences.** Each slot plays a sequence script (`rsBattleAnimationRunScript`): poses with their frames, and the commands `$E8`-`$FF` in [BattleAnimation.h](../../include/BattleAnimation.h). `rsBattleAnimationReadStep` reads each step word minus 1, so command `$FF` is compared as `$FE`. A pose's delay is scaled by the slot's 8.8 multiplier (`rsBattleAnimationScaleDelay`).
- **Drawing.** `rlBattleAnimationDrawSlot` draws the pose at the slot's screen position (mirrored by its H-flip bit) and, on a new pose, queues its frame's tile rows (`rlBattleAnimationLoadFrameTiles`). `rsBattleSceneDrawShadows` draws each combatant's first frame record at its ground position on alternate frames.
- **Actions and moves.** An action (`rlBattleAnimationQueueAction`) is a byte of the class's move program: a move list or a single sequence. Bits 0-1 of `$0AF5,x` pick its variant (`ANIM_CMD_ED`). Moves wait in `aBattleSceneMoveQueues` (16 per combatant) until the combatant stands ready (`rsBattleAnimationTakeMove1`/`2`); an empty queue sets its bit of `bBattleSceneQueuesIdle`. A missed strike makes the defender dodge (`rlBattleSceneQueueDodge`, action 1).
- **Movement.** The move lists' steps run the move routines in [animation_moves.asm](../../engine/battle_scene/animation_moves.asm). `rsBattleAnimationMoveRight`/`Left` stop a combatant against the other's body (`rsBattleAnimationCheckBlocked`) or the stage edge, and scroll the camera after it: toward the midpoint of the attacker's and defender's X (`lBattleSceneCameraAttackerX`/`DefenderX`, long pointers), or the attacker's alone. `rsBattleSceneTurnCombatants` turns idle combatants to face each other.
- **Release signals.** `ANIM_CMD_F4` freezes a combatant's sequence; the other side signals it (`$F3`, or its effect slot ending) through `bBattleSceneRelease1`/`2`, and `rlBattleAnimationRunSignals` moves its script past the `$F4`.
- **Knockback.** A strike knocks the defender back (`rlBattleSceneSetKnockback`): `bBattleSceneKnockback` holds a strength of `$762` / its class's weight (`aBattleSceneClassWeights`), taken to 3/4 each frame as it pushes the combatant (`rlBattleSceneRunKnockback`), then `$18` frames of sideways shake.
- **Spawning an effect.** Sequence command `$EF` starts the combatant's effect slot (`rsBattleAnimationCmdSpawnEffect`). The new slot is built in a fifth record, the spawn slot at `$0BFF` (`bBattleAnimationSpawnStateFlags`...): filled from `aBattleAnimationSpawnSlotTemplate`, placed at the thrower plus its class's offset (`aBattleAnimationEffectStarts`, one list each for bows, hand axes and javelins), aimed at the defender (`rsBattleAnimationAimEffect`: X speed, Y step to the defender's hit height, a jump arc), and copied into slot 2 or 3 (`rlBattleAnimationCopySpawnSlot`).
- **Body records.** Each combatant has an 8-byte body record chosen by class and weapon category (`rlBattleAnimationSetBodyIndices`): 0 its width, 1 the height its effects aim at and fly from, 2 the distance it keeps, 3 and 4 two speeds, 6 half its starting distance; 5 and 7 are read only by code nothing reaches.
- **Effect slots.** `rlBattleAnimationSetEffectMotion` picks the motion by the attacker's `AnimStyle` and weapon; each frame `rlBattleAnimationMoveEffectSlot` moves the slot by it: straight flight with gravity and a pose by its slope, a path (`aBattleAnimationEffectPaths`), or a path script with commands `$8080`-`$8085` (`aBattleAnimationEffectPathScripts`). Reaching the defender lands the strike (`rlBattleSceneApplyEffectStrike`); landing or leaving the stage ends the slot (`rlBattleAnimationEndEffectSlot`).
- **Afterimages.** Sequence command `$EC` 1 starts one of two afterimages (`aBattleSceneAfterimages`): the owner's pose drawn 8 or 12 frames back. `$EC` 3 draws the slot mirrored around the defender on odd frames (`rlBattleAnimationGetMirroredX`).
- **Sounds.** On a river or in the sea's water, footsteps `$11`-`$19` become splashes `$21`-`$29` (`rsBattleAnimationUseWaterSounds`); with a weapon of `aBattleSceneListedWeapons` (`BattleSceneFlagListedWeapon1`/`2`), sounds `$47`, `$6D`, `$8C` and `$8E` become the next one (`rsBattleAnimationUseListedWeaponSounds`).

## Script routines

Script commands 4, 5 and 8 take an index into `aBattleSceneScriptRoutines` (72 long pointers in bank `$97`): call once, call every frame until it clears bit 0 of `$7FE4B7`, or set as the routine run every frame (`lBattleSceneScriptRoutine`). Entries 1, 2 and 13 are the experience bar and the level up window above; the rest are screen/window fades, stat-gain/level-up floating sprites (`rlBattleSceneScriptInitStatGains`, `rlBattleSceneScriptInitLevelUpNumber`), portrait palette loaders, and effect helpers.

## Depends on

- **The battle calculation** (bank `$84`, [engine/battle/battle.asm](../../engine/battle/battle.asm)): the action structs and `aBattleRoundsData`.
- **The core:** the HDMA engine, the IRQ engine (`rlIRQEngineCreateEntry`), the decompressor, DMA, sound (`rlPlaySoundEffect2`, `rlGetSoundPanFromXPixel`), the dialogue engine for the messages.
- **Used by** the title screen (its effects and `wBattleSceneFrame`) and the demo battles.

## Open questions

- **Battle type 2.** Class change (promotion): launched by `rlStartCastleClassChange` ([class_change.asm](../../engine/castle/class_change.asm)) with the old class in `aActionStructUnit1`, the promoted class in `aActionStructUnit2`, and `$FFFF` in `aBattleRoundsData._Entry`. It clears the quotes and drops, has no foreground, uses background `$17` and scripts `$33`/`$35`, and returns to the castle screen like type 3.
- **`BattleSceneFlagSolo2`** is set for the dance and for Fortify (both combatants at the center) and turns on HDMA entries 3 and 6. What those draw is not known.
- **`bBattleSceneRanged`.** It is never stored: `rsBattleSceneStartNextRound` loads the ranged bit into A and branches past the store.
- **Extra tiles ID 0** copies `$C0` tiles from `$86E000` (`ANIM_NO_EXTRA_TILES_SOURCE`), event data in the released ROM. Whether that VRAM is ever shown is not known.
- **The magic sword's spell rows.** Bits 3-5 of `bBattleAnimationCombatantFlags` pick one of eight rows of `aBattleSceneMagicSwordSpellActions`, but only `rlBattleSceneRunSpellRowCommand`, which nothing reaches, sets a row other than 0.
- **`rlBattleAnimationGetEffectArcHeight`** reads the defender's height with X = `$0E` for combatant 1's effect, an offset into `aBattleSceneCombatant1`, not into the slots. It reads the wrong byte; what difference that makes on screen is not known.
- **Data tables.** All 790 routines in `engine/battle_scene/` are named; the window effect data tables (`aTitleIntroScene6WindowEffect1Data` at `$97/F64E`, `aTitleSceneWindowEffectData` at `$97/A7C7`) are named for their readers, but their format is not described yet.
- **Graphics sources.** The 231 reachable class, extra, weapon and character-piece sheets are in `gfx/battle_animation/` as tile sheets in upload order; `tools/battle_animations.py` builds and validates them and draws the frames from the sources. A class sheet's PNG layout comes from the `ANIM_TILE_ROW` slices in `frames_*.asm`. The upload queue (`rlBattleSceneQueueUpload`) holds 16 entries and `rsBattleAnimationQueueFrameRows` ignores its full flag, so a frame's rows past what fits are dropped; the original frames queue at most two.
