# Battle

Combat resolution, stat calculation, skills, weapon traits, staff effects, scripted criticals, and battle banners. Map-battle playback (`map_battle*.asm`, `map_magic_effects.asm`, `map_action_animations.asm`) is in [map_battle.md](map_battle.md), the full battle scene in [battle_scene.md](battle_scene.md), and the arena wrapper in [arena.md](arena.md). Guide chapters: [6, Resolving a battle](../guide/06-resolving-a-battle.md) and [7, Growing stronger](../guide/07-growing-stronger.md). Per-routine details: [routines/battle.md](routines/battle.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## Data and RAM

- `aActionStructUnit1` (`$7E/4EB5`) and `aActionStructUnit2` (`$7E/4F15`): 96-byte `structActionStructEntry` records ([include/Battle.h](../../include/Battle.h)). Player and allied units always go into `Unit1`; enemy and neutral units go into `Unit2`.
- `wBattleType` (`0` normal, `1` arena, `2` class change), `wBattleStatus` (bit 0 = initiator side, bit 1 = sides swapped, `$10`/`$20` = defeated, `$40` = animations off), `wBattleDistance`, `wBattleSong`, `wBattleCriticalType`.
- `aBattleRoundsData` (`$7E/4F81`): 4-byte struct header + up to 80 two-byte entries (`bBattleRoundFlags` + damage/HP byte). Sentinels: `$02FE` (Pavise), `(skill << 8) | $FE` (sword skill), `$FEFF` (end of arena exchange), `$FFFF` (end of battle).

## Control flow

1. **Map action dispatch:** `rlRunMapAction` dispatches `wMapActionTargetType` through `aMapActionRoutines` (`rsMapActionBattle`, `rsMapActionHelp`, `rsMapActionGiveGold`, `rsMapActionTalk`).
2. **Forecast (`rlActionStructGetBattleStats`):**
   - Clears battle RAM (`$7E/4EA9`–`$7E/5024`), assigns `Unit1`/`Unit2`, computes distance (`rlGetBattleDistance`), and gathers basic data (`rlActionStructGetBasicBattleData`).
   - Calls [battle_stats.asm](../../engine/battle/battle_stats.asm) getters (temporarily applying the evaluated weapon's stat bonuses), populates `TriggerableSkills` (`CombatSkillFlag*`), sets 100% crit on class effectiveness, applies weapon triangle (`rlApplyWeaponTriangleBonus`, ±20 Hit/Avoid) and 3-tile auras (`rlApplyCharmAndLeadershipHitAvoidBonus`), halves might against Loptous unless negated (`rsSetHalvedBattleMight`), and checks scripted crits for the initiator (`rlActionStructGetScriptedCritPossibility`).
3. **Round resolution (`rlActionStructBuildBattleRounds`):**
   - Determines first striker (`wBattleStatus & 1`, or higher Attack Speed in the arena via `rsActionStructGetBattleInitiatorIfArena`).
   - On the first exchange (`wChapterFlags & $0010` clear), rolls `Vantage` (`rsActionStructAdjustVantageRoundOrder`), handles `Dance` (`rsActionStructCheckForDance`), and clears silenced magic (`rsActionStructClearMagicEquipmentIfSilenced`).
   - Loops rounds: initiator strikes, defender counters, `Pursuit` doubler strikes (`rsActionStructCheckIfCombatantsCanDouble`), then either writes `$FEFF` (arena) or checks `Accost` (`rsActionStructCheckForAccostBattleRound`).
   - Each call to `rsActionStructAddStrikes` rerolls sword skills (`Astra`, `Luna`, `Sol`, `Holy`, `Darkness`), checks `Adept` or `WeaponTraitBrave` (`rsActionStructGetNumberOfStrikes`), and runs each swing through `rsActionStructResolveStrike` (`aActionStructWeaponTraitEffects` or `aActionStructStaffEffects`).
   - Ends with `$FFFF`, runs `rsActionstructGetLevelUpAndDroppedItemData` for both sides, updates Canto/action end, triggers battle events (special crit action `$1D`, battle action `$14`), and hands off to `rlStartCombatAnimation` (`rlStartMapBattle` or `rlStartBattleScene`).

## Open questions and retail quirks

- **Adept negative-AS loop:** In `rsActionStructGetNumberOfStrikes`, if a unit with `Adept` has `BattleAttackSpeed + 20 < 0`, `bmi -` branches back to the Astra check and loops indefinitely (unreachable in vanilla without a weight exceeding speed + 20).
- **Last-hit EXP:** Each connecting hit overwrites `GainedExperience` instead of adding to it; non-lethal arena hits award none.
- **Unreached staff effect:** `rsActionStructStaffEffectRefresh` (`$84/EB24`) is not listed in `aActionStructStaffEffects`.
