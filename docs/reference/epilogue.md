# The epilogue

What happens after the final chapter's last event: each region's and character's ending on the world map, the second generation's character endings, the staff credits, the end screen, the play record and the ranking screen. Per-routine details: [routines/epilogue.md](routines/epilogue.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## How it runs

The final chapter's ending event calls `rlStartEpilogue`: `wEpiloguePart` 0, no current chapter, and a fade to `rsOpenEpilogueScreen`. That main loop routine sets game states `$36`/`$35`/`$37` (`rlEpilogueSetup`, `rlEpilogueUpkeep`, `rlEpilogueStep`) and starts the part in `wEpiloguePart`. `rlEpilogueStep` runs one step a frame by `wGameStateStep` (`aEpilogueStepRoutines`). Two steps are shared: `EPILOGUE_STEP_FADE_OUT` and `_FADE_IN` change the brightness one level every 8 frames and then go to the step left in `wNextMenuState`, so most steps end with "fade, then step N".

| Part | Started by | Steps |
|---|---|---|
| 0, the endings | `rlBeginEpilogueEndingsEvaluation`: works out each ending (`rlEvaluateEpilogueEndings`) | 12 starts the world map epilogue event (`aEventChapterEpilogueWorldMap`); 5 runs while it plays; 0 and 1 show a text page for 192 frames |
| 3, character endings | `rlStartEpilogueCharacterEndings` | 8, 9: one scene per second generation character (25-63, `wEpilogueCharacter`) through the castle screen's conversation scene; holding B quits to the title. After the last, part 1 |
| 1, the credits | `rlSetupEpilogueCreditsDisplay` (to step 13 straight away when the final ranking score is not 0) | 2, 3, 4: the credits text prints into BG3 while two backgrounds scroll up at different speeds, with six sprites and two HDMA effects (layers switched partway down, BG2's lines swaying); then 13 and 14, the end screen, for 48 seconds or until a button |
| 2, the play record | `rlSetupEpilogueRankingsDisplay` | 6, 7: the play record scrolls (each chapter's turns, the total, then every recruited unit's wins and losses, grey if dead); then 15-17, the ranking screen |

The routines that move from part to part (`rlStartEpilogueCredits` and `rlStartEpilogueCreditsQuickly` at the end of the world map, in [world_map.asm](../../engine/epilogue/world_map.asm), and `rsStartEpilogueCharacterScene`) set `wEpiloguePart` and fade back to `rsOpenEpilogueScreen`.

## The play record and the ranking screen

The play record is one long dialogue (`dialogueEpilogueCredits8DC9FF`) printed into a text window (`rlSetUpEpilogueRecordTextWindow`). Its ASMCs fill in the numbers: `rlASMCRecordTurnsPrologue`... `rlASMCRecordTurnsFinal` put a chapter's turn count (`aChapterTurncounts`) in `DIALOGUE_NUMBER1` and push spaces to right-align it (`rsAlignRecordNumber2`), `rlASMCRecordTurnsTotal` the sum. `rlASMCRecordNextGeneration1Unit` and `...Generation2Unit` are loops written as ASMCs: each finds the next recruited character, sets its name, wins and losses (`rlGetCharacterWinCount`, `rlGetCharacterLossCount`) and pushes a line of text followed by the ASMC itself again, until the last character.

The ranking screen (`rlShowEpilogueRankingScreen`) shows the tactics, experience, survival and combat rankings and the final score ([engine/rankings/](../../engine/rankings/)), the play time in hours and minutes, the number of times the save has finished the game, and 16 letters from `aUnknown7FC70A`. After 384 frames one more row is uploaded, and then the game waits for the secret code (`aEpilogueSecretCode`: Select, Up, A, Down, B, Left, X, Right, Y, L, R, Start). Entered in full, it uploads a hidden row of the screen and the epilogue stops at step 18 for good. A wrong button starts the code again.

## Depends on

- **Events and dialogue**: the world map epilogue is an event; the credits, the record and the endings are dialogue with ASMCs.
- **Castle** ([castle.md](castle.md)): the character endings use its conversation scene.
- **Rankings and save** ([save.md](save.md)): the ranking calculations, the win and loss counts, the play time and the completion count.
- **World map** (bank `$8B`, not documented yet): the world map epilogue and the moves between parts.

## Open questions

- What the 16 letters at `aUnknown7FC70A` are, and what the hidden row revealed by the secret code shows (the screen alone can settle both).
- `rlSetupEpilogueCreditsDisplay` skips the credits text and goes straight to the end screen when the final ranking score is not 0; whether that is what happens on a normal ending.
- `aEpilogueCharacterSceneDialogue` points every character at one of two texts (`dialogueEpilogueCredits8DCC6B` for characters 25-39, `8DCC94` after); the scene's real text must come from elsewhere (the conversation scene's own lookup).
- Three debug button handlers are unreached (`rsEpilogueDebugButtonsUnused`, `rsEpilogueDebugButtons2Unused`, and `rlSetUpEpilogueTextWindowUnused`'s caller), and `aUnknown8DADFA`'s HDMA info is unused.
- Step 5 draws three of the credits sprites during the world map event; what they are on screen.
