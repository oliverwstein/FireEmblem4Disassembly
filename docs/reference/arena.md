# The arena

The castle arena menu, opponent lookup, battle setup, and reward/yield handling. Per-routine details: [routines/arena.md](routines/arena.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## Files

| File | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| [arena.asm](../../engine/arena/arena.asm) | `rlArenaScreenSetup`, `rlArenaMenuRunStep` (the 16-step `aArenaMenuStepHandlers` state machine), `rsDrawArenaOpponent`, opponent selection (`rlGetArenaOpponentCharacterID`, `rlGetArenaOpponentEquippedWeapons`), `rlStartArenaBattle`, and `rlGetArenaBattleResult` |

## Menu flow

`rlArenaScreenSetup` decompresses the arena graphics (`aArenaDecompressionEntry0000`, `2800`, `2C00`, `4000`), clears the castle list/item windows, installs `aUnknown8AB230` HDMA, and sets `wGameStateStep` to `CASTLE_STEP_ARENA`. Each frame, `rlArenaMenuRunStep` uploads `aBG3TilemapBuffer` to VRAM `$7000` and dispatches `wMenuState` through `aArenaMenuStepHandlers`:

- **Entry (`rsArenaMenuStepInit`, step 0):** Checks `rlGetSelectedUnitArenaLevel`. If level ≥ 7, shows the arena-cleared message and exits (`rsArenaMenuStepExitArena`); otherwise opens the welcome dialogue and moves to `rsArenaMenuStepFightOrQuit` (step 4).
- **Fight / Quit / Weapon select (steps 4, 12, 13):** `rsArenaMenuStepFightOrQuit` offers Fight or Quit. Fight checks the unit's inventory and opens the weapon list (`rsArenaMenuStepOpenWeaponList`, `rsArenaMenuStepSelectWeapon`), then previews the opponent (`rsArenaMenuStepShowOpponent`, `rsArenaMenuStepStartQuitChangeWeapon`).
- **Starting the fight (`rsArenaMenuStepFadeAndStartBattle`, step 11):** Fades `bBufferINIDISP` to 0, records current gold in `$0AD9`, increments `$0AD7`, and calls `rlStartArenaBattle`.
- **After the fight (`rsArenaMenuStepAfterBattle`, step 2):** Reads `rlGetArenaBattleResult`:
  - `0` (win): calls `rlHandleArenaExitEvent`, animates the prize gold count-up (`rsArenaMenuStepWaitGoldDialogue`, `rsArenaMenuStepCountUpGold`), and either shows the champion message (level ≥ 7) or prompts for the next round (`rsArenaMenuStepConfirmNextRound`, step 7).
  - `1` (loss): calls `rlHandleArenaExitEvent`, shows the defeat message, and exits (`rsArenaMenuStepExitAfterLoss`, step 15).
  - `2` (yield/draw): shows the yield message (`rsArenaMenuStepAfterYield`, step 3) and offers Leave, Continue, or Change Weapon (`rsArenaMenuStepLeaveContinueWeapon`, step 8).

## Opponents and battle setup

- `rlGetArenaOpponentCharacterID` reads `rlGetChapterArenaOpponentsPointer` (`structChapterEventData`), chooses the melee or ranged opponent list based on `rlGetSelectedItemRange`, indexes by `rlGetSelectedUnitArenaLevel`, and substitutes the Gen 2 replacement if the Gen 1 unit is not in RAM.
- `rlGetArenaOpponentEquippedWeapons` fetches the opponent's equipped weapon ROM pointers for display in `rsDrawArenaOpponent`.
- `rlStartArenaBattle` creates the opponent unit at RAM slot `$2100` (unless `wChapterFlags` bit `$10` is already set), builds the battle rounds (`rlActionStructBuildBattleRounds`), sets `wBattleSong = $3B`, and launches `rlStartBattleScene` (`wBattleType = 1`).
- `rlHandleArenaExitEvent` (in [engine/events/event_engine.asm](../../engine/events/event_engine.asm)) cleans up `$2100`, clears `UnitStateMiracleActive`, heals survivors to max HP, and triggers event action `$1C` (or clears `DeploymentStateDefeated` on a loss).
