# The arena

The castle town's arena: the menu, the opponent lookup, starting a fight, and reading its result. The battle engine's arena rules are in [battle.asm](../../engine/battle/battle.asm) and the battle scene's in [scene.asm](../../engine/battle_scene/scene.asm). Guide chapter: [16, The arena](../guide/16-the-arena.md). Per-routine details: [routines/arena.md](routines/arena.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## Menu

`rsCastleEnterFacility` saves the unit in `wCastleSavedUnitRAMPointer` and clears `wArenaFightsWithOpponent`. `rlArenaScreenSetup` starts song `$4B`, loads the graphics and keeper, and lists the unit's equippable weapons (`rlBuildEquippableWeaponList`).

| 2 | `rsArenaMenuStepAfterBattle` | the result: win (prize line, steps 5-6, then 7 or the champion line), loss (step 15), yield (step 3) |
| 3 | `rsArenaMenuStepAfterYield` | the yield line, step 8 |

## Opponents and fights

- `rlGetArenaOpponentCharacterID`: the melee list for a weapon with minimum range 1, the ranged list for 2 or more; entry = arena level; if a unit with that character exists, the next entry (Chapter 2: Keimos after a recruited Chulainn).
- `rlStartArenaBattle`: unless `wChapterFlags` `$10` is set (a fight in progress), builds the opponent at unit header `$2100`, which is in no army's list. Then the stats, `wBattleType` 1, the rounds, battle song `$3B`, the battle scene (always the full scene).
- The round builder: the faster unit strikes first each exchange; skills and Vantage are set up only on the first; each exchange ends with `$FEFF`, and only a fatal strike ends with `$FFFF` and clears `$10`. The battle scene asks for the next exchange unless B was pressed (`BattleSceneRoundArenaRetreat`) and builds it in its main loop (`rlBattleSceneBuildArenaBout`).
- Other arena rules: a fatal strike is always fatal; a player unit at 0 HP keeps 1 HP; a kill pays the prize (1,000 + 500 × level, capped at 50,000 money) and raises the level instead of recording a win; experience only from the fatal strike; no stealing; a broken weapon keeps swinging; the unit's action doesn't end.
- `rlGetArenaBattleResult`: 1 if the unit's row is defeated, 0 if its action struct has a prize, 2 (yield) otherwise. After a yield the opponent, its HP and `$10` remain until the unit leaves or wins.
