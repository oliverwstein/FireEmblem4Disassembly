# The title screen

The title screen is game states `$1A`/`$1B`. It plays an intro of about two minutes: eight illustrated scenes with captions at the bottom, then the title logo built from sprites. Then it waits for Start. Start goes to the file menu. If nobody presses it, the attract mode's opening ([demo.md](demo.md)) starts again, and that opening ends by coming back here.

The screen borrows the battle scene's engines for its palette effects, window effects and screen registers (banks `$95`/`$96`), which is why it sat in those banks. Until wave 5 it was also misnamed the "map battle" (`rlMapBattleSetup`, `GAMESTATE_MAP_BATTLE_*`, `OPEN_MAP_BATTLE`).

Per-routine details: [routines/title.md](routines/title.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## Files

| File | Responsible for |
|---|---|
| [title_screen.asm](../../engine/title/title_screen.asm) | Opening the screen (bank `$80`), its VBlank, main loop and IRQs, the setup and the step with its three phases (bank `$9B`). Also the engines the phases share: the timeline interpreter, the load list, the title sprites, the VBlank routines, the edge fade and the caption strip. |
| [title_scenes.asm](../../engine/title/title_scenes.asm) | What the timelines call: each intro scene's registers, graphics, scrolling and effects; the brightness fades; the logo sprites; the music; the fade out's steps (banks `$95`, `$9B`). |
| [title_timelines.asm](../../engine/title/title_timelines.asm) | The three timelines (bank `$97`). |
| [include/TitleScreen.h](../../include/TitleScreen.h) | The timeline macros (`TITLE_TIMELINE_*`), `structTitleSprite` and the `TITLE_SPRITE` definition macro. |

The title's RAM is the block at `$7FD000` in [WRAM.h](../../include/WRAM.h) (`bTitleScreenPhase`, `wTitleScreenFrame`, `lTitleTimelinePosition`, `aTitleTimelineSlots`, `aTitleSprites`, ...). The battle scene reuses that area, so `tools/name_ram.py` writes those names only in `engine/title/`.

Its data in other banks:
- the sprite definitions, frames and still scripts (`aTitleSpriteDefinitions`, `aTitleSpriteFrames`, `aTitleSpriteStill00`..., bank `$99`), still in `engine/unsorted/bank9B.asm`;
- the brightness lists and load lists (`aTitleSceneFadeInBrightness`, `aTitleLoadList`, bank `$97`), in `bank97.asm`;
- the caption scrolls (`aTitleCaptionScrolls`).

## How it is reached

- **After the attract mode's opening plays to the end.** The opening's last event command, `OPEN_TITLE_SCREEN` (`$5E`), flashes the screen white with sound `$84` (`rlFlashToTitleScreen`, run by the event engine's hook). Then `rsOpenTitleScreen` opens the title screen with its intro.
- **When the opening is skipped with Start** (`DEMO_ALLOW_SKIP`, `procDemoSkipFadeOut`), `rsOpenTitleScreenWithoutIntro` opens it without the intro. `rsOpenTitleScreen` does the same when Start is already held (`rlTitleScreenIsStartHeld`). Both set `bTitleScreenSkipIntro`.

Both entries reset the engines, decompress the title's graphics into `$7F0000` (`rlDecompressTitleGraphics`), set the screen registers (`rlSetTitleScreenRegisters`) and two IRQs at lines 6 and 189 (`aTitleScreenTopIRQ`, `aTitleScreenBottomIRQ`). They install `rsRunTitleScreenVBlank` and `rsRunTitleScreenMainLoop`, and set game state `$1A`.

## The game states and phases

`rlTitleScreenSetup` (`$1A`, once) clears the procs, HDMA and the battle scene's effect state.
- **Without the skip flag:** it sets up the intro (`rlSetUpTitleIntro`, `rlLoadTitleIntroGraphics`), plays song 1 (`rlPlayTitleIntroSong`) and starts phase 0 with `aTitleIntroTimeline`.
- **With the skip flag:** it goes straight to the title (`rlTitleScreenSkipIntro`, `rlTitleScreenShowTitle`).

`rlTitleScreenStep` (`$1B`, every frame) runs the phase in `bTitleScreenPhase` and counts `wTitleScreenFrame`.

| Phase | Routine | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 0, the intro | `rlTitleScreenRunIntro` | Runs the timeline and the slot routines (`rsRunTitleTimeline`, `rsRunTitleTimelineSlots`), the load list, the battle scene's effect engines and the title sprites; sets the edge brightness. **Start skips the intro:** sound `$0A`, then `rlTitleScreenSkipIntro` blanks the screen and switches to `aTitleTimeline`. Once the decompressions and sound uploads have finished, `rlTitleScreenShowTitle` loads the title (`rlLoadTitleGraphics`, the logo, song 3) and sets phase 1. If the intro plays to its end, its own timeline builds the title and sets phase 1 (`rlEnterTitlePhase`, frame `$186D`). |
| 1, the title | `rlTitleScreenRunTitle` | The same engines. Once `rlAllowStartOnTitle` has run, Start (`rlTitleScreenIsStartPressed`) calls `rlTitleScreenStartFadeOut`: sound `$1E`, phase 2, `aTitleFadeOutTimeline`. |
| 2, the fade out | `rlTitleScreenRunFadeOut` | Runs the fade out's timeline. At its end (`bTitleScreenDone`), it clears the game state and calls `rlReturnToFileMenu`. |

In phases 0 and 1, when the timeline ends (`rlEndTitleScreen` sets `bTitleScreenDone`), the step calls `rlStartOpeningEvent` and the attract mode's opening plays again. In the intro this happens at frame `$1C9F` (about two minutes). After skipping to the title it happens at frame `$041E` of `aTitleTimeline`. Neither `rlTitleScreenIsStartPressed` nor `rlTitleScreenIsStartHeld` accepts Start while `wMusicID` is not 0 (a song change is waiting).

## Timelines

A timeline (`TITLE_TIMELINE_*` in [TitleScreen.h](../../include/TitleScreen.h), written from the ROM by `tools/title_timeline.py`) is a list of entries. Each entry is a frame number and commands, run once `wTitleScreenFrame` reaches that frame:

- `TITLE_TIMELINE_SLOT n, routine` installs a routine in slot `n` of `aTitleTimelineSlots`. It then runs every frame, with X = the slot's offset, until it clears the slot's flag. Most installers run their step once and store the step's address in the slot, so a slot holds a small state machine.
- `TITLE_TIMELINE_CALL n, routine` calls a routine once, with A = `n`.

The interpreter is `rsRunTitleTimeline`, and `rlResetTitleTimeline` restarts it. The intro uses the slots by convention:

| Slot | Use |
|---|---|
| 0 | Brightness fades. Each installer points `lTitleBrightnessList` at a list of INIDISP values (`aTitleSceneFadeInBrightness`...); `rlRunTitleBrightnessList` applies one value a frame. |
| 1 | The scene's background scrolling (`rlStartTitleIntroScene1Scroll`...). |
| 2-4, 6, 8 | HDMA and palette effects. |
| 7 | The caption: fades it in and out. |

`CALL 2, rlAllowTitleLoad` appears throughout. Each call lets the load list (`aTitleLoadList`, run by `rlRunTitleLoadList`) start one more decompression or copy of the next scene's graphics, so the loads are spread over the scene before.

## The intro

These are the frames at which `aTitleIntroTimeline` sets up each scene.

| Frame | Scene setup | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| `$0001` | (`rlSetUpTitleIntro` and `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene1`, at setup) | Fade in. Slot 4 runs an HDMA wave (`rlUnknown9BE5B2`). |
| `$05FA` | `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene2` | Sprites from `$082A`: six pairs started by `rlUnknown95F9CE`... with palette fades. |
| `$0C26` | `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene3`, `$0C46` `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene3b` | |
| `$0C62` | `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene4` | |
| `$0D6E` | `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene5` | |
| `$0EFE` | `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene6` | Song 2 at `$10F2` (`rlPlayTitleIntroSong2`). |
| `$11FB` | `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene7` | From `$1237`: rotating sprites (`rlUnknown9BE2A4`, `rlUnknown9BE435`). Slot 6 (`rlUnknown9BF49E` at `$1302`, `rlUnknown9BF57A` at `$14B9`) steps through 33 palettes that `rlUnknown9BF3F3` interpolated into `$7F8000` at setup. |
| `$1719` | `rlSetUpTitleIntroScene8` | A sprite moving along `aTitleSprite0Motion` with an afterimage, from `$1859`. |
| `$1873`-`$1889` | `rlStartTitleLogoSprite1`...`11` | The title logo, one sprite every frame or two. `rlAllowStartOnTitle` follows, then the BG3 effects and song 3. |

**Captions.** Before each new scene, slot 7 fades a caption in (`rlStartFadingInTitleCaption`) and later out. `rlShowNextTitleCaption` moves on to the next caption: it sets the caption strip's horizontal scroll from `aTitleCaptionScrolls`. The strip is drawn mid-frame by the bottom IRQ (`rlDrawTitleCaptionStrip`): below line 189 it switches the PPU to BG1 alone at `bTitleCaptionBrightness`. The text is graphics, not encoded text.

**The edge fade.** While `bTitleScreenEdgeFade` is set, the screen starts blanked. The IRQs then raise the brightness over the first and last lines in steps (`rlFadeTitleScreenTopEdge`, `aTitleScreenEdgeBrightness`, computed by `rlSetTitleEdgeBrightness`).

**Title sprites.** There are 13 of them (`aTitleSprites`, `structTitleSprite`). Each is started from a 13-byte definition (`TITLE_SPRITE`, `rlStartTitleSprite`) and animated by a two-byte step script (a frame and its duration; `$80` and a count jumps back). `rlUpdateTitleSprites` animates and draws them every frame. A sprite can draw an afterimage from a 16-entry ring of its past positions (`rlStartTitleSpriteAfterimage`, `rlDrawTitleSpriteAfterimage`).

## The fade out

`aTitleFadeOutTimeline` dims the palettes to half (`rlStartDimmingTitle`), changes a few registers over 50 frames, and ends the screen (`rlEndTitleScreenFadeOut`). It also starts the fade-out load list (`rlStartTitleFadeOutLoadList`).

## Not reached

These routines are built, but nothing in the build reaches them. Most are slot installers and steps no timeline names, each noted "nothing in the build reaches this": `rlRestartTitleIntro`, `rlUnknown95F35D`, `rlUnknown95F37D`, `rlUnknown95F5D9`, `rlUnknown95F68B`, `rlUnknown95FC51`, `rlUnknown95FDE4`, `rlUnknown95FFAC`, `rlUnknown9BE2E3`, `rlUnknown9BE331`, `rlUnknown9BE8D7`, `rlUnknown9BF1F4`, `rlStopTitleSprite`. The sprite data at `$9B/FE13` (`aUnknown9BFE13`) is also unreferenced.

## Depends on

- **The battle scene's engines** (banks `$95`/`$96`, `engine/battle_scene/`): `rlBattleSceneWriteRAM` sets screen registers from `WRITE_RAM_*` lists. `rlBattleSceneStartPaletteEffect` runs palette effects (scripts in `$97/A9A8` and `$99`), and `rlBattleSceneStartWindowEffect1`/`2` runs window effects. Also used: `rlBattleSceneScriptClearLoading`/`UpdateLoading`, and helpers still `Unknown` (`rlUnknown95C368`, `rlUnknown96D5DB`, `rlUnknown96D659`, `rlUnknown96D695`, `rlUnknown96CB44`, `rlUnknown96A532`, `rlUnknown95F14D`, `rlUnknown95F741`, `rlUnknown95F7F7`). These belong to the battle scene wave.
- **The core:** the HDMA engine (`rlHDMAEngineCreateEntryByIndex`), the IRQ engine (`rlUnknown82E0C6`), the decompressor and decompression list (`rlAppendDecompList`), the fades (`rlFadePaletteToBlack`, `rlFadePaletteToColors`), sound (`wMusicID`, `rlPlaySoundEffect`).
- **The attract mode:** `rlStartOpeningEvent` and the event engine's hook into `rlFlashToTitleScreen`.
