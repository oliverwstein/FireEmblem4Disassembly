## [animation_moves.asm](../../../engine/battle_scene/animation_moves.asm)

Battle animation move routines: a move list step whose movement is below $7D runs one of these, through aBattleAnimationMoveRoutines (rlBattleAnimationStartMovement), and the path routines they use.  Each combatant (X = 0 or $43) has a position in 8.8 pixels: X at $0AF8 (the whole pixels at $0AF9, 9 bits), Y at $0AFD (whole pixels at $0AFE; the ground is $58). A move sets some of: $0B00   target X ($0AFF its fraction) $0B08   X speed, 8.8 pixels a frame, bit 15 leftward ($0B0C keeps the start) $0B0A   X deceleration a frame ($FFFF: halve the speed each frame) $0B0E   vertical velocity, $0B10 gravity (rlBattleAnimationGetJumpArc) $0B04   target Y, $0B13 Y step a frame and each frame rlBattleAnimationUpdateX, rlBattleAnimationApplyDefaultGravity, rlBattleAnimationUpdateJump and rlBattleAnimationUpdateYStep apply them. Distances and speeds come from the combatant's body record (the table at $97/EA9A: width, spacing, speeds A and B).
