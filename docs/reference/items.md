# Items, inventories, and staves

Global item instances, ROM item templates, per-unit inventories, equip/range checks, pricing, broken weapons, and staff targeting/healing. Per-routine details: [routines/items.md](routines/items.md) (generated by `tools/reference.py`).

## Data structures

- **ROM templates (`aItemData`, 138 entries):**
  - `structItemROMEntry` (10 bytes): `Name`, `EquipmentType`, `Durability`, `Cost`, `StatBonuses`, `StaffEffect`, `WeaponRank`, `Skills`. Used by rings (`EquipTypeRing = 2`) and `Circlet`.
  - `structWeaponStaffROMEntry` (19 bytes): extends `structItemROMEntry` with `WeaponType`, `Might`, `Accuracy`, `Weight`, `MinRange`, `MaxRange`, `Effectiveness`, `Traits`, `AdditionalInfo`. Used by weapons (`EquipTypeWeapon = 0`) and staves (`EquipTypeStaff = 1`).
  - Weapon-only getters in `items.asm` (`rlGetItemWeaponType`, `rlGetItemMight`, `rlGetItemAccuracy`, `rlGetItemWeight`, `rlGetItemMinimumRange`, `rlGetItemMaximumRange`, `rlGetItemEffectiveness`, `rlGetItemWeaponTraits`, `rlGetItemAdditionalInfo`) check `EquipmentType >= EquipTypeRing` and return `0` so they never read past a ring's 10-byte entry.
- **Global item instances (`aItemRAMData` at `$7E/3D87`):** `structRAMStructHeader` followed by 256 × 6-byte `structItemRAMEntry` records (`ItemID`, `Durability`, `ItemState`, `OwnerRAMPointer` / `OwnerID_InventorySlot` union used during save/load, `KillCount`). `rlInitiateItemRAMData` zeroes the block, builds the header, appends all 140 entries from `PlayerItemTable.csv` in state `ItemStateUnobtained` (`7`), and sets `PI_Tyrfing`'s durability to `0`.
- **`ItemState` values:** `1` (`ItemStateInventory`), `2` (`ItemStateSupply`), `4` (`ItemStateShop`), `5` (`ItemStatePawnbroker`), `7` (`ItemStateUnobtained`). `3` (`ItemStateUnknown3`) and `6` (`ItemStateUnknown6`) are unused in `engine/`.
- **Unit inventories (`aInventoryRAMArea` at `$7E/3664`, 73 slots):** Each `structInventoryDataRAM` holds `EquippedWeaponBitfield` (1 byte, MSB-first: slot 1 = `$80` down to slot 8 = `$01`), `structRAMStructHeader`, and 8 `Slot` bytes (1-indexed; player UI caps inventories at 7). `rlSaveCharacterInventoryToBuffer` unpacks an inventory into the word-wide `structInventoryDataBuffer`.
- **Player vs. enemy slots:** `UnitTypeStatic` and `UnitTypeDynamic` store 1-based `aItemRAMData` indices (*Player Item IDs*). `UnitTypeHoly` and `UnitTypeEnemy` store raw ROM `ItemID`s directly and zero `wCurrentItemDataRAMPointer` on lookup (`rlGetItemIDByInventorySlot`, `rlGetItemROMDataByInventorySlot`); only enemy `Drops` reference `aItemRAMData`.

## Item instances, durability, kills, and pricing (`items.asm`)

- **Pool management:** `rlAppendNewItemRAMEntry` creates a `structItemRAMEntry` with durability from ROM max (or `0` when `wR0 == 5` / `ItemStatePawnbroker`). `rlGetItemRAMStructEntryByPlayerItemIndex` resolves a 1-based index into `wCurrentItemDataRAMPointer`. `rlClearItemRAMDataEntryUnused` overwrites an entry with `aEmptyItemRAMEntry`. `rlRevertItemAcquirement` resets an entry to `ItemStateUnobtained`, owner `0`, and ROM max durability.
- **Durability and broken weapons:** `rlReduceItemCurrentDurability` decrements non-zero durability and returns `sec` only on the strike that drops it to `0` (`clc` if still `> 0` or already `0`). When `Durability == 0`, `rlGetEffectiveItemIDByItemRAMStructEntry` and `rlGetEffectiveItemROMPointerByItemRAMStructEntry` substitute `rsGetBrokenWeaponID` via `aBrokenWeaponOffsets` and `aBrokenWeaponData`.
- **Kills:** `rlGetItemRAMKillCountAndCritAbility` returns `KillCount` in `A` and `sec` if `>= 50`. `rlAddToItemRAMKillCount` adds `A` and clamps at `100`.
- **Pricing:** `rlGetItemBuyPrice` returns `Cost` if unused or unbreakable (`$FF`), `1000` (`$03E8`) if broken (`Durability == 0`), or `2 * rlGetItemSellPrice` if partially used. `rlGetItemSellPrice` computes `(Cost / 2) * CurrentDurability / MaxDurability`; `rlGetItemRepairCost` computes `Cost * (MaxDurability - CurrentDurability) / MaxDurability`; `rlGetItemRepairCostPerUse` computes `Cost / MaxDurability`. Callers apply Bargain halving.
- **Save-load clamping:** `rsCapItemDurability` (called by `engine/save/save.asm`) caps `ValkyrieStaff`, `SleepStaff`, and `BerserkStaff` at 1, `SilenceStaff` at 3, `EarthSword` at 10, and forces `Circlet` to `$FF`.

## Inventories, equipping, and range (`inventory.asm`)

- **Giving and removing items:** `rlLoadEquipmentDataIntoSpecifiedInventory` initializes the `structRAMStructHeader`, clears `EquippedWeaponBitfield`, loads `Equipment1`/`Equipment2`, checks `UniqueEquipment`/`Drops` via `rlCheckForValidUniqueItemOrDrops`, and calls `rlEquipFirstWeaponInInventory`. `rlGiveItemToUnit` dispatches by `UnitType` (`aUnitTypeItemGivingPointers`) to `rsGivePlayerItemIDToSelectedUnit` (sets `ItemState = 1`, `OwnerRAMPointer = wSelectedUnitDataRAMPointer`, applies ring bonus, auto-equips if none equipped) or `rsGiveItemToSelectedEnemyUnit` (appends raw ROM `ItemID`, applies ring bonus). `rlRemoveItemInSpecifiedInventorySlot` removes the slot, clears any ring stat bonus (`rsRemoveItemStatBonusIfRing`), and updates `EquippedWeaponBitfield` (`rsUpdateEquippedWeaponBitfieldIfSlotChanged`).
- **Ring stat bonuses:** `aRingStatBonusTable` maps `StatBonuses` IDs `1..7` (Speed, Magic, Power, Shield, Barrier, Leg, Skill rings) to `RingStatBonusBitfield` bits (`$10`, `$40`, `$80`, `$08`, `$04`, `$02`, `$20`).
- **Equippability and obtainability:**
  - `rlCheckWeaponEquippable`: requires `EquipTypeWeapon`, matches `GenderFemale` if `WeaponTraitFemaleOnly`, requires non-zero max durability and not `StatusSilence` for magic weapons (`>= WeaponTypeStaff`), checks unit weapon rank against item rank, and for `WeaponRankHoly` scans `aHolyBloodGrowthBoostsOffsets` for `MajorHolyBlood` (broken holy weapons with max durability `0` pass immediately).
  - `rlCheckStaffEquippable`: rejects `StatusSilence`, requires `EquipTypeStaff` and non-zero max durability, checks `WeaponTypeStaff` rank, and shares `_HolyWeapon` for `WeaponRankHoly`.
  - `rlCheckItemObtainable`: rings always pass; weapons/staves check `WeaponTraitFemaleOnly` and rank, while `WeaponRankHoly` items always pass.
- **Range and counterattacks:** `rlGetSelectedItemRange` returns `wR10` (min) and `wR11` (max, `+1` if `UnitStateAssault`). `rlGetInventoryWeaponsRangeFlags` scans slots `1..8` and returns bitflags (`aWeaponRangeFlags`) for equippable weapons in `A` and offensive staves (`StaffEffect >= $0C`, when not Silenced) in `Y`. `rlGetCounterableWeaponSlot` returns the first slot covering range `A`, or falls back to `rlGetFirstEquippableWeaponInInventory`.

## Staves (`staves.asm`)

`StaffEffect` (`$00`–`$10`) indexes four parallel jump tables for target checking and target selection:

| Mode | Target check | Target selection |
|---|---|---|
| Map | `aStaffTargetChecks` (`rlCheckStaffHasTargets`) | `aStaffTargetSelectionHandlers` (`rlStartStaffTargetSelection`) |
| Castle | `aCastleStaffTargetChecks` (`rlCheckStaffHasCastleTargets`) | `aCastleStaffTargetSelectionHandlers` (`rlStartCastleStaffTargetSelection`) |

- **Effects:** `$01`–`$03` Heal/Mend/Recover, `$04` Physic, `$05` Fortify, `$06` Return, `$07` Warp (sets `wChapterFlags` bit `$0800`), `$08` Rescue, `$09` Charm (0 max durability, so never equippable), `$0A` Restore, `$0B` Valkyrie, `$0C` Silence, `$0D`–`$0F` Sleep/Berserk/Thief (Thief also has 0 max durability), `$10` none. `wChapterFlags` bit `$0020` indicates inside a castle.
- **Target filters** passed to `rlCheckStaffTargetsInRange` / `rlBuildCastleTargetList`: `$02` same army, `$08` missing HP, `$09` has status, `$0C` magic user with lower resistance, `$0D` enemy with lower resistance.
- **Healing:** `rlGetStaffHealAmount` returns `Magic + 10` (Heal, Physic, Fortify), `(Magic + 10) * 2` (Mend), or `80` (`$50`, Recover). `rlApplyFortify` heals every unit in `aTargetDistanceMap` on the map (`rsApplyFortifyOnMap`) or every unit in `aCastleUnitDeploymentOffsets` except `aActionStructUnit1` in the castle (`rsApplyFortifyInCastle`).
- **Valkyrie:** In the castle (`rsCastleStaffValkyrie`), spawns `procMainLoopChangeFade` to `rsReviveScreenOpen`.

## Dependencies

- `core/`: RAM struct list helpers (`rlBuildRAMStructHeader`, `rlAppendNewStructEntry`, `rlLoadRAMStructDataByIndex`, `rlSaveRAMStructDataByIndex`, `rlRemoveRAMStructEntryByID`, `rlFindRAMStructEntry`, `rlGetRAMStructCurrentStructCount`, `rlGetRAMStructEntryPointerByStructIndex`, `rlWriteToFirstStructEntry`), math (`rlUnsignedMultiply16By16`, `rlUnsignedDivide32By32`, `rlUnsignedDivide16By16R13`), and procs (`rlProcEngineCreateProc`).
- `units/`: unit type, gender, status, states, magic, weapon ranks, Holy Blood checks, ring bonus setters, equipped-weapon stat bonus sync, and HP healing.
- `map/` & `castle/`: range target search (`rlFindTargetsInRange`, `rlBuild32x32MapUnitGrid`), `rlStartChoosingTarget`, `rlBuildCastleTargetList`, `rlCastleStartStaffTargetSelection`, `rlCastleUseStaffFromMap*`, and `rsReviveScreenOpen`.
- `battle/`: `rlCheckForValidUniqueItemOrDrops`, `rlRunMapAction`, and `aActionStructUnit1`.
- `save/`: item serialization/deserialization and `rsCapItemDurability`.

## Disassembly notes and anomalies

- **Retail bugs in unused code (`items.asm`):** `rlGetWeaponEffectivenessTextUnused` (`$87/E5C5`) calls itself recursively instead of `rlGetItemEffectiveness`; `rlGetItemMaxDurability1` and `rlGetItemMaxDurability2` are identical duplicates; `rlGetItemZeroUnused` and `rlStaffUnusedStub` are unreachable stubs.
- **Equip/rank omissions (`inventory.asm`):** `rlGetFirstEquippableWeaponInInventory` checks `EquipTypeWeapon` without checking weapon rank; `rlGetCounterableWeaponSlot` checks range without checking equippability.
