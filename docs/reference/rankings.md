## Score scale

All five routines return a 0-based grade:

| Value | Grade |
|---|---|
| `0` | A (best) |
| `1` | B |
| `2` | C |
| `3` | D |
| `4` | E (worst) |

`rsEvaluateSpecifiedRankingScore` (`$84/8ED7`) loops `X = 0..3` over four `(min, max)` word pairs, returning `X` on the first interval where `val >= min` (tested via `min - 1 < val`, signed) and `val <= max` (unsigned). Values outside all four intervals fall through to `4` (E).

## Categories and thresholds

| Category | Routine | Source metric | A (`0`) | B (`1`) | C (`2`) | D (`3`) | E (`4`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Tactics** | `rlCalculateTacticsRanking` (`$84/8CFE`) | Sum of 12 `aChapterTurncounts` words (Prologue–Final), then `+ wDestroyedLocationsCount` clamped to `<= 4` | `1..399` | `400..549` | `550..799` | `800..1099` | `1100+` or `0` |
| **Survival** | `rlCalculateSurvivalRanking` (`$84/8D3F`) | `wTotalAlivePlayerUnitCount` (living `FS_Player` units at end of Chapter 5 + Final Chapter) | `44..48` | `43` | `42` | `41` | `<= 40` |
| **Combat** | `rlCalculateCombatRanking` (`$84/8D65`) | Sum of all 48 entries of `aUnitLossesCount` (`rlGetLossCountByIndex`) | `0..3` | `4..10` | `11..30` | `31..50` | `51+` |
| **Experience** | `rlCalculateExperienceRanking` (`$84/8D9B`) | `wTotalLevelUpCount` | `1000..1440` | `800..999` | `600..799` | `400..599` | `< 400` or `> 1440` |

## Final score

`rlCalculateFinalRankingScore` (`$84/8DC1`) averages the four category scores, rounding half up:

```
final_score = (tactics + survival + combat + experience + 2) >> 2
```

For example, three A's (`0`) and one B (`1`) sum to `1`; `(1 + 2) >> 2 = 0` (overall A).

## Dependencies

- **Events and map (`rlSaveChapterTurncount`, `map_actions.asm`):** records `aChapterTurncounts`, `wTotalAlivePlayerUnitCount` (at Chapters 5 and 11), and `wDestroyedLocationsCount`.
- **Units (`level_up.asm`):** increments `wTotalLevelUpCount`.
- **Save (`save.asm`):** tracks `aUnitLossesCount` and persists `score + 1` into `aSRAM.wRankingScore`.
